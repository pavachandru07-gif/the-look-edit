THE EDITOR'S NOTE

Style is in the details.

Fashion is more than following trends. It is the art of understanding proportion, texture, colour and personality. Learn the language, then make it your own.

A Few Fashion Rules Worth Knowing

Little details that can make a difference to your styling.

01. Proportion

Balance oversized and fitted pieces to create interesting silhouettes. A loose shirt with tailored trousers is one example.

02. Texture

Mixing different textures can make a simple outfit feel more considered. Think satin with knitwear or denim with leather.

03. Personal Style

Trends come and go. Build a wardrobe around pieces, colours and silhouettes that feel authentic to you.
