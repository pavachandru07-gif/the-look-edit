# ✦ THE LOOK EDIT

### The Art of Personal Style.

**THE LOOK EDIT** is a fashion and beauty editorial website designed for individuals who love exploring fashion, discovering trends, experimenting with personal style and expressing their individuality.

A digital destination where fashion meets creativity, inspiration and self-expression.

---

## ✨ About the Project

THE LOOK EDIT brings together the worlds of fashion, beauty and personal styling in one interactive platform.

From runway inspiration and haute couture to everyday outfits, beauty discoveries and personalised style recommendations, the platform encourages users to explore and develop their own fashion identity.

## 💎 Features

* **Discover Your Style** – Interactive style discovery quiz.
* **The Runway Edit** – Explore runway fashion and designer collections.
* **The Trend Report** – Discover emerging fashion trends.
* **Haute Couture** – Explore exceptional craftsmanship and high fashion.
* **The Beauty Edit** – Skincare, makeup and beauty inspiration.
* **Screen to Street** – Fashion inspiration from films and pop culture.
* **My Closet** – Organise your digital wardrobe.
* **Outfit Planner** – Create and save outfit combinations.
* **Style Calendar** – Plan outfits for upcoming occasions.
* **Wishlist** – Save your favourite fashion and beauty finds.
* **Fashion Finder** – Discover fashion brands.
* **Style Moodboard** – Collect and organise visual inspiration.
* **Style Dictionary** – Explore fashion terminology.
* **Style Challenge** – Try creative styling challenges.
* **Style Studio** – Experiment with outfit combinations.
* **Designer Spotlight** – Discover influential designers.
* **Fashion News** – Explore fashion industry stories.
* **Beauty Finder** – Discover beauty inspiration.
* **Shopping Edit** – Explore shopping inspiration.
* **The Lookbook** – Browse curated outfit ideas.
* **The Occasion Edit** – Find outfit inspiration for different occasions.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

THE LOOK EDIT follows a contemporary editorial fashion aesthetic.

The visual identity combines:

* Vibrant colours and elegant typography.
* Fashion-inspired editorial layouts.
* Curated visual references.
* Clean and responsive page designs.
* A balance of luxury, creativity and modern minimalism.
